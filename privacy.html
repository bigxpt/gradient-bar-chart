Privacy Policy

BIGXPT: Gradient Progress Chart
Effective date: 30 September 2026

Overview

BIGXPT: Gradient Progress Chart is a Tableau Viz Extension. The extension processes Tableau-provided data in the user's Tableau environment solely to render and format the visualization.

Workbook and user data

The extension does not independently transmit Tableau workbook data to BIGXPT-operated servers and does not store workbook data in a BIGXPT external database.

Tracking and advertising

The extension does not contain advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking, or profiling code.

Local preferences

The extension may use browser local storage for limited interface preferences, such as the last active settings tab and remembered formatting-dialog geometry. These preferences are not sent to BIGXPT.

Third-party platform services

The extension operates within Tableau and loads the Tableau Extensions API from Tableau's hosted domain. Tableau's own services and policies may apply independently of BIGXPT.
